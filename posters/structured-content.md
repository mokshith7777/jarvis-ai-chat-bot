## Design Instructions

### Style Preferences
- **Layout**: dense-modules (single hero composition with 7 integrated modules)
- **Style**: cyberpunk-neon + pop-laboratory hybrid — neon glow on dark with technical precision
- **Aspect**: 16:9 (landscape cinematic)
- **Resolution**: 7680×4320 (8K UHD)
- **Color grading**: midnight lift, gold midtones, cyan highlights, neon accents — DaVinci Resolve filmic LUT feel

### Layout Preferences
- Center-anchored reactor (hero)
- HUD brackets at 4 corners (frame)
- Neural web + scanlines (atmosphere)
- Vignette (visor)
- Text hierarchy bottom-weighted (42%-68% viewport height)
- Deployment pills at bottom edge

### Other Requirements
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: static reactor, no scanline drift, vignette opacity 0.03
- Chromatic aberration only on highlights >90% luminance
- 16-bit color pipeline throughout (no banding)
- Export: PNG (lossless) + WebP (95%) + JPEG (92%)
- Social crops: 16:9 (hero), 9:16 (Stories/Reels), 1:1 (profile), 4:5 (Instagram feed)
